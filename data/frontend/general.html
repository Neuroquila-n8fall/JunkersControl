Low-setpoint shutdown

A sustained effective feed setpoint of 10 °C (50 °F) is the manufacturer's off request. Heating is disabled after this grace period even if an external client continues to send Enabled.

Offline Fail-safe

Used only when MQTT remains disconnected beyond the configured grace period. Command frequency does not affect fail-safe mode.
