°C feed / °C outside
Strength of the next 3-hour trend: cooling raises the feed target; warming lowers it. The correction is limited to ±2 °C.
How forecast support works
The trend is the average hourly forecast temperature for the next 3 hours minus the same weather provider's current temperature, not the boiler's outside sensor. The coefficient sets the feed-temperature change per degree of this trend.
In valve mode, a negative correction is allowed only when all relevant rooms have no heat deficit. Boost, direct override, off and hold ignore forecasts. The boiler's physical maximum still applies.
Enabling this setting does not provide a forecast sample. Only fresh samples affect heating; the correction stops when the sample expires. Samples are cleared on restart.
