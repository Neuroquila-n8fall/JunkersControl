How to read these values

The weather limit applies only during normal heating in dynamic valve mode: 0% removes weather support; 100% restores the full curve contribution.

Home Assistant supplies the forecast sample. The raw trend is the next 3-hour forecast average minus the weather provider's current temperature. Cooling raises the request; warming lowers it, with a correction limited to ±2 °C. Disabled support, expired or missing samples, boost, direct override, off and hold do not apply a forecast correction. A dash means the value is unavailable.

The sample's room flag permits a lower feed request in valve mode. It is not a measurement or confirmation of physical room comfort.

Weather and forecast support do not bypass the boiler's physical maximum for feed requests. This is a request limit, not a guarantee of measured water temperature.
